FG Announces New Student Loan Update for 2026

By Kayblog | May 13, 2026

The Federal Government has announced new updates to the student loan scheme to make it easier for Nigerian students to access funding.

What changed? The application portal is now fully digital, no need for long bank queues. Interest rate remains zero while you are in school.

Who can apply? Any student in federal university, polytechnic or college of education with JAMB admission letter and NIN.

How to apply: Visit nelf.gov.ng, create account, upload school ID, admission letter and NIN. Wait for approval in 48 hours.
