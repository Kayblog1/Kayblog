Top 10 Nigerian Songs Trending This Week

By Kayblog | May 13, 2026

Music in Nigeria right now is on fire! From Afrobeats to Amapiano, here are the top 10 songs everyone is listening to this week.

1. Burna Boy - City Boys - Still topping charts with massive streams.

2. Ayra Starr - Commas - The queen is back with another hit.

3. Davido - Feel - Davido never disappoints.

4. Asake - Lonely At The Top - An anthem for the streets.

5. Omah Lay - Reason - Smooth and vibey.

6. Rema - Calm Down - Still a global hit.

7. Wizkid - IDK - Wizkid with that classic vibe.

8. Tems - Love Me JeJe - Sweet vocals.

9. Kizz Daniel - Twe Twe - The dance floor killer.

10. Shallipopi - Cast - The new street anthem.
