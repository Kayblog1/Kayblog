How To Start Importation With Just 100k

By Kayblog | May 13, 2026

You don't need 1 million to start importation. With 100k you can start small and grow.

Step 1: Download 1688 app and use Google Translate for Chinese to English.

Step 2: Find products like phone pouch, mini gadgets, wigs accessories that cost $1-$2.

Step 3: Get a trusted shipping agent on Facebook - charge per kg (about 13k per kg).

Step 4: Start with 10-15kg, sell on WhatsApp, Jiji and Instagram with 100% profit.

Many students are making 200k monthly from this. Start small!
